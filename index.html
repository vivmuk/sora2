Sora 2 Pro Tips & Tricks

Expand to master Sora 2's advanced capabilities with these essential techniques.

Cinematic Prompts

Sora 2 responds exceptionally well to detailed production-style briefs. Treat your prompt like a director's slate with duration, resolution, shutter, lens, filtration, grade, lighting, and sound.

Multi-Step Instructions

Sora 2 tracks world state across shots. Write sequences rather than single moments—it keeps objects and characters consistent throughout complex narratives.

Physics Awareness

Describe actions with realistic cause-and-effect (missed shots that rebound, buoyant boards that flex, gymnastic routines). Sora 2 is notably better at obeying physical laws.

Audio Synchronization

Prompt for synchronized dialogue, sound effects, and background soundscapes. Include who speaks, when, and what the environment should sound like.

Cameos Feature

Record your likeness once in the app, then drop yourself (or friends) into any scene—appearance and voice are preserved. Great for rapid, personal storytelling.

Include Failures

Because Sora 2 models failure (not just success), you can prompt beats where things don't work (a miss, a slip, a wobble) to enhance realism and narrative.

Iterate & Refine

Expect big leaps in realism—but not perfection. The model still makes mistakes. Iterate and refine prompts, especially on edge-case physics or highly specific details.
