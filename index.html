Official Sora 2 Prompt Structure

Based on the official Sora 2 guide. Use this 9-step template for maximum control, realism, and audio synchronization.

1. Primary Intent

One sentence: What happens and why (emotional or narrative purpose)
Example: Deliver scale and tactile realism while showing creature motion.

2. Shot Sequence (Ordered)

Numbered shots with timecode, action, beats, continuity:
Shot 1 (0.0–3.5s): [Action and camera movement]
Shot 2 (3.5–7.0s): [Include natural failure/miss/wobble for realism]
Shot 3 (7.0–10.0s): [Resolution with clear cause→effect]
💡 Sora 2 maintains world state across cuts

3. Look & Format

Resolution, frame length, camera sensor emulation, shutter, film grain
Example: 10.0s; 4K; 180° shutter; large-format sensor emulation; fine grain

4. Camera & Lens Notes

Lens: Focal length; Rig: handheld/drone/gyro; Motion: track/push/arc
Example: 50mm spherical; gyro-stabilized drone; parallel track with slight inward arc

5. Lighting & Atmosphere

Key direction, time of day, weather, particulate effects
Example: Late-afternoon cross-key; thin frozen haze; rim warm amber; breath vapor visible

6. Surface & Costume Reads

Material finishes and microdetail cues that affect lighting
Example: Semi-iridescent scales with micro-frost on leading edges; worn leather jacket with water beading

7. Sound & Dialogue Timing

Diegetic sounds: [with timing]
SFX: [specific sounds synced to actions, timestamps]
Dialogue: [exact line, who speaks at X.Xs, cadence]
Score: [present/absent + style]
💡 Audio synchronization is a core Sora 2 feature - always specify timing precisely

8. Finishing & Grade

Grain, halation, dynamic balance, poster frame
Example: Very fine grain; minimal halation; preserved multiband dynamics; poster frame at 4.2s

9. Safety / Cameo Controls

If using real people: Include cameo verification and consent controls
Example: "cameo: upload-verify-allowed; only [user] may grant reuse; revoke with ID"
💡 Sora 2 supports accurate likeness insertion with consent controls

Sora 2 Pro Tips & Tricks

Expand to master Sora 2's advanced capabilities with these essential techniques.

Cinematic Prompts

Sora 2 responds exceptionally well to detailed production-style briefs. Treat your prompt like a director's slate with duration, resolution, shutter, lens, filtration, grade, lighting, and sound.

Multi-Step Instructions

Sora 2 tracks world state across shots. Write sequences rather than single moments—it keeps objects and characters consistent throughout complex narratives.

Physics Awareness

Describe actions with realistic cause-and-effect (missed shots that rebound, buoyant boards that flex, gymnastic routines). Sora 2 is notably better at obeying physical laws.

Audio Synchronization

Prompt for synchronized dialogue, sound effects, and background soundscapes. Include who speaks, when, and what the environment should sound like.

Cameos Feature

Record your likeness once in the app, then drop yourself (or friends) into any scene—appearance and voice are preserved. Great for rapid, personal storytelling.

Include Failures

Because Sora 2 models failure (not just success), you can prompt beats where things don't work (a miss, a slip, a wobble) to enhance realism and narrative.

Iterate & Refine

Expect big leaps in realism—but not perfection. The model still makes mistakes. Iterate and refine prompts, especially on edge-case physics or highly specific details.
